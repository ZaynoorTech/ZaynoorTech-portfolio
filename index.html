01 — About

Technology, creativity and practical problem-solving.

ZaynoorTech is an independent studio building clean, functional digital products for businesses and individuals.

The work sits where technology, creativity and practical problem-solving meet. Sometimes that means a website that loads fast, reads clearly and turns visitors into enquiries. Sometimes it means a small Python tool that removes a repetitive task, or visuals that make a brand look like it means business.

I'm a developer and digital creator building my practice across front-end development, Python and design, with a structured focus on software development, AI engineering and cybersecurity. In practice, that means honest scoping, clear communication, and work delivered at a standard I'd put my name on — including being straight with you about what I can and can't take on.

I work remotely with clients wherever they are, and I'm most useful to small businesses, founders and creators who need something built properly without a long agency process.
